In nineteen seventy-seven, a spacecraft about the size of a small car left Earth.
Its name was Voyager One.
It flew past Jupiter.
Then Saturn. And it kept going.
In nineteen ninety, from six billion kilometres away, it turned around and took one last picture of home. A pale blue dot, smaller than a single pixel.
In twenty twelve, it crossed into interstellar space. The first human-made object ever to do it.
Today it is more than twenty-four billion kilometres away. A radio message takes almost a day to reach it.
Its power is fading. One day, it will fall silent.
But it carries a golden record. Music, and greetings from Earth, for anyone who finds it.
